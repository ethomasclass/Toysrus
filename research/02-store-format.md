# 1990s Toys "R" Us store format — size, exterior, floor plan, signage

## Size
- Corporate prototype: **46,000 sq ft** (SEC 10-K FY1995/98/99: "46,000 square feet prototype design, with 30,000 and 20,000 square feet stores opened in smaller markets"). Roughly 35–40k sales floor plus a rear stockroom.
- Ceiling: 1980s builds had exposed deck; 1990s builds/remodels had a white perforated 2×4 lay-in drop ceiling with continuous fluorescent strips. Steel gondola shelving "reached to the ceiling," implying ~14' clear with ~12' racking.
- Floor: white 12" VCT tile, buffed glossy. Walls: white painted block. The 2025 film *Roofman* rebuilt a store with 23,000 VCT tiles.
- Concept 2000 (July 1996, Raritan NJ): glass front instead of opaque brick, blue facade with yellow-lined windows, oval/racetrack aisles, lower center fixtures, dedicated Barbie/LEGO/video game sections, Bike Shop, customer information center. 89 stores by Jan 1998, 97 by Jan 1999. C-3 (1998–2000, Alpharetta GA test): full racetrack, "worlds," carpeted Learning Center and Baby's Corner, glass-enclosed R Zone.

## Exterior (freestanding prototype, for contrast with Huntsville)
- Brown "quasi-mansard" rippled roof fascia; vertical rainbow-stripe painted planks across the front (standard red, blue, green, yellow repeating; regional variants).
- Channel letters in rainbow colors with the mirrored R (star inside the R from 1986). Orange-brown ENTRANCE sign with Geoffrey, rainbow EXIT ONLY sign, yellow/blue WELCOME sign, brown pylon road sign.
- Protruding glass vestibule with vending machines; some stores had an interior ramp.
- Kids "R" Us was often an attached combo store (15,500–21,500 sq ft).

## Interior layout (warehouse prototype, 1990–95)
- **Supermarket grid**: long parallel gondola aisles running front-to-back, a wide front action alley behind the checkouts, a rear alley along the back wall. Floor-to-ceiling steel racking with cased overstock on the top shelves.
- **Aisle signs**: hanging blue panels with white text and a letter+number code (e.g. "6C" in 1992 footage; "C5 – Big Wheels, Radio Flyer, Smart Trikes", "F12 – Dr. Brown's, Playtex, Infant Feeding" on surviving signs).
- **Front**: bank of checkout lanes with conveyor belts, customer service/returns/layaway near the entrance, the enclosed video game pickup booth by the exit.
- **Video games**: World of Nintendo / later "R" Zone. A wall of laminated box-front sleeves you lift to read the back, each with a clear/blue pouch of paper slips (yellow or white). Take a slip, pay at the register, present the receipt at the **big blue pickup booth** with a small window and a steel door to the stockroom. Consoles in locked glass cases; Nintendo demo kiosks and a giant Game Boy kiosk. Replaced by locked clamshells in 1999–2000.
- **Bikes ("Bikes R Us")**: assembled bikes hung from wall/ceiling racks along the back wall, Big Wheels and Radio Flyers below; you took the tag to the register and waited at "CUSTOMEЯ PICK UP."
- **Baby/Infants corner**: diaper pallets, formula, cribs, strollers, car seats in a rear corner (aisle F codes).
- **LEGO**: blue carpet in the LEGO area (Carbaugh). **Barbie**: the hot-pink aisle. Little Tikes/Fisher-Price large plastics assembled on the floor. Seasonal (pools, swing sets, Power Wheels) near the back.
- Concept 2000 additions used in the model: Geoffrey floor medallion in the front action alley, low center gondolas, blue/yellow department blades, Geoffrey statue.

## Signage & palette
- Interior palette: white shell, royal blue (#1F4FBF) and yellow (#FFD200) for counters, header panels and blades; rainbow graphics; orange price channels on shelf edges; white shelf-edge labels with black dot-matrix price; yellow sale cards.
- Geoffrey (90s design): tan/yellow giraffe with brown spots, often in a red-and-white striped shirt; on the WELCOME sign, Birthday Club poster, carts and baskets.
- Jingle: "I don't wanna grow up, I'm a Toys R Us kid" (1982, J. Walter Thompson).
