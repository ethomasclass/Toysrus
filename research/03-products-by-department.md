# What was on the shelves, 1993–1998 (by department)

Packaging conventions: **peg walls of blister cards** (action figures, Hot Wheels/Matchbox, Micro Machines, Polly Pocket, Littlest Pet Shop, small Nerf, Tiger handhelds, batteries, most under-$10 items) in 4-ft bays 6–8 rows high; **boxes on shelves** (dolls in window boxes, vehicles/playsets, LEGO, board games face-out, preschool, RC); **floor/pallet** presentation for Power Wheels, Little Tikes, swing sets, pools, bikes, diaper cases. Video games were never on the open floor.

## Video games / R Zone
SNES ($129.99, grey/purple box; DKC bundle), Sega Genesis Model 2 ($99 Vectorman bundle 1996, black/red), Nintendo 64 (Sept 1996, $199.99, red/black; TRU-exclusive gold N64), PlayStation (1995, grey), Sega Saturn (1995, $199.99 w/ 3 games 1996), Game Boy / Pocket (1996) / Color (1998), Game Gear, Virtual Boy (1995–96, clearance by 1997), Tiger R-Zone (1995). Titles on the flap wall: Donkey Kong Country ($59.99), Mortal Kombat II ($72.99), Super Mario 64, Mario Kart 64, GoldenEye 007, Sonic 3, Crash Bandicoot, Final Fantasy VII (1997), Pokémon Red/Blue (Sept 1998). Accessories on pegs: Game Genie, Super Scope, Rumble Pak, memory cards, Mad Catz; Nintendo Power on a wire rack. Demo kiosks: Nintendo preview kiosks, SNES/Super Game Boy, N64 pods, PlayStation and Saturn units, giant Game Boy kiosk.

## Action figures (pegged, 5")
Power Rangers (Bandai 1993–98; Megazord/Dragonzord boxes; 8" figures $10), TMNT (Playmates, green sewer-brick cards), Batman Animated/Forever/& Robin (Kenner), Star Wars POTF2 (Kenner 1995–98, orange then green cards; X-Wing, Millennium Falcon boxes), X-Men/Spider-Man (ToyBiz), Spawn (McFarlane 1994+), WWF Hasbro (1990–94) then Jakks, G.I. Joe (to 1994; Extreme 1995–97), Street Sharks, Jurassic Park (1993; Lost World 1997), Toy Story (Thinkway), Gargoyles, Biker Mice, Stretch Armstrong.

## Dolls / the pink aisle
Barbie (Totally Hair 1992, Hollywood Hair, Dr. Barbie, Holiday Barbie on a glass-fronted shelf, TRU exclusives), Dreamhouse/Motorhome/Corvette on top shelves, Polly Pocket (Bluebird), Cabbage Patch Kids (Hasbro then Mattel), Baby Alive, Sky Dancers (Galoob 1994–97), Littlest Pet Shop (Kenner), Magic Nursery, Water Babies, Puppy Surprise, Spice Girls (1998), Pretty Pretty Princess, Dream Phone, Mall Madness. American Girl was mail-order only.

## Building sets
LEGO System (yellow boxes): Town, Pirates (to 1997), Castle (Royal Knights 1995, Fright Knights 1997), Space (Ice Planet, Spyrius, UFO), Aquazone (1995), Western (1996), Adventurers (1998); Technic; DUPLO. Lit glass case with a built model at the aisle end. K'NEX (Big Ball Factory 1994), Lincoln Logs, Tinkertoy, Mega Bloks, Erector.

## Games & puzzles
Milton Bradley: Mouse Trap, Hungry Hungry Hippos, Candy Land, Chutes and Ladders, Connect Four, Battleship, Operation, Guess Who?, Dream Phone, Mall Madness, Crossfire, 13 Dead End Drive, Don't Wake Daddy, Jumanji (1995). Parker Brothers: Monopoly ($9.99 in 1991), Sorry!, Clue, Trivial Pursuit, Risk, Ouija. Pictionary, Twister, Perfection, Bop It (1996), UNO, Magic: The Gathering, Pokémon TCG (1999), 1000-piece puzzles.

## Preschool
Fisher-Price (Little People, Corn Popper, Chatter Telephone, Great Adventures castle), Playskool (Mr. Potato Head, Sit 'n Spin, Weebles, Glo Worm, Talk 'n Play), Tickle Me Elmo (Tyco 1996, $28.99, sold out chain-wide), Little Tikes assembled on the floor (Cozy Coupe, Turtle Sandbox, Party Kitchen, Log Cabin), Barney, Teletubbies (1998), Blue's Clues, Etch A Sketch, Lite-Brite, View-Master.

## Bikes / ride-ons / outdoor
Huffy and Murray bikes hung on racks (Barbie/Power Rangers licensed bikes), Roadmaster, Power Wheels (Barbie Jeep, Jeep Wrangler, Kawasaki ATV) in giant boxes plus a floor model, Big Wheel, Radio Flyer, Rollerblade, Skip-It, Pogo Ball, Moon Shoes, Hedstrom swing sets, Intex pools, Slip 'N Slide, Grow-to-Pro basketball, Stomp Rocket.

## Electronics / RC
Tyco RC (Rebound 4x4 1997, Fast Traxx, Turbo Hopper, Jet Turbo Python $44.99, Mutator), Nikko, Tiger LCD handhelds on pegs, Talkboy ($29.99), Talkgirl, Giga Pets (1997), Tamagotchi (1997, $18), Nano Pets, Furby (Nov 1998, $35), Bop It, Laser Challenge, Lazer Tag, Yak Bak, Dr. Dreadful, Creepy Crawlers, VTech laptops. Batteries on a clip strip at every aisle end.

## Plush / collectibles
Ty Beanie Babies (in TRU 1997–99; bins on end-caps), Puffkins, Pokémon plush (late 1998), Fleer/Topps/Upper Deck cards, Marvel cards, Pogs (1994–95), Trolls, Lion King, Toy Story Buzz/Woody, Gund/Applause plush wall, Yomega yo-yos (1998).

## Baby / juvenile
Pampers/Huggies/Luvs on pallets, Similac/Enfamil/Carnation, Gerber, Playtex, Graco strollers and Pack 'n Play, Century/Evenflo car seats, Cosco/Kolcraft high chairs, Exersaucer (1994), Diaper Genie, cribs in flat boxes, Johnson & Johnson.

## Sporting goods
Super Soaker 50/100/XP 150 ($19.99 1995)/CPS 1000–2000 (1996–98), Nerf Bow 'n' Arrow, Sharpshooter, Ballzooka, Crossbow, Turbo football, Koosh, Wiffle, Franklin, Spalding/Wilson balls in wire bins, Bell helmets.

## Diecast
Hot Wheels (99¢, blue cards), 5-packs, Criss Cross Crash, Matchbox (Tyco), Micro Machines (Galoob; Star Wars 1994+; Z-Bots), Racing Champions NASCAR, Tonka steel trucks, Nylint, Buddy L.

## Crafts
Play-Doh (Fun Factory, Mop Top), Crayola 64 with sharpener, Super Art Desk ($15.99 1994), Model Magic, Rose Art, Creepy Crawlers, Shrinky Dinks, Spirograph, Lite-Brite, Magna Doodle, Colorforms, Sand Art, Gak/Floam.

## Checkout & Geoffrey
Candy rails (Skittles, M&M's, Reese's, Nerds, Ring Pop, Push Pop, Big League Chew), PEZ, Hot Wheels singles, Tamagotchi, trading-card packs, batteries, Geoffrey Dollars gift certificates, film and disposable cameras, Koosh, Slinky, bubbles, Geoffrey plush and balloons, Geoffrey Birthday Club sign-up, walk-around Geoffrey on weekends, orange baskets and blue-handled carts.
